[Directions Q1-Q10]: Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions that follow.

The digital transformation of the Indian banking sector has accelerated dramatically over the past five years. Driven by rapid smartphone penetration, cheap high-speed data, and the groundbreaking Unified Payments Interface (UPI) developed by the NPCI, India has emerged as a global leader in retail digital transactions. However, this sudden paradigm shift presents dual challenges: cybersecurity vulnerabilities and financial exclusion among non-tech-savvy rural demographics. While commercial banks are heavily investing in AI-driven fraud detection systems, regulators emphasize that customer awareness and robust compliance frameworks are equally crucial to maintaining systemic stability. Furthermore, the introduction of Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) by the Reserve Bank of India aims to streamline cross-border payments and reduce cash printing costs, marking the next frontier in monetary innovation.

Q1. Which of the following best expresses the primary objective of the passage?
(A) To analyze the rapid growth, challenges, and future innovations in Indian digital banking
(B) To criticize commercial banks for failing to prevent online fraud
(C) To argue that physical currency should be completely abolished immediately
(D) To compare Indian banking growth with European banking models
(E) To advocate for higher transaction fees on UPI payments
Correct Option: A
Explanation: Paragraph 1 outlines growth, paragraph 2 addresses challenges like cybersecurity, and paragraph 3 highlights CBDC innovations.

Q2. According to the passage, what dual challenges accompany India's digital banking surge?
(A) High inflation and declining interest rates
(B) Cybersecurity vulnerabilities and financial exclusion in rural areas
(C) Shortage of bank branches and lack of currency notes
(D) Strict regulatory fines and falling credit demand
(E) None of the above
Correct Option: B
Explanation: The passage explicitly states dual challenges of cybersecurity vulnerabilities and financial exclusion among non-tech-savvy rural demographics.

Q3. Which factor did NOT directly contribute to the rapid growth of retail digital transactions in India?
(A) Smartphone penetration
(B) Affordable high-speed internet data
(C) Unified Payments Interface (UPI)
(D) Mandatory closure of physical bank branches
(E) NPCI payment infrastructure
Correct Option: D
Explanation: Mandatory closure of physical branches is nowhere mentioned as a driver.

Q4. Choose the word MOST SIMILAR in meaning to "PARADIGM" as highlighted in the passage.
(A) Pattern / Framework
(B) Anomaly
(C) Stagnation
(D) Obstacle
(E) Misconception

Explanation: Paradigm shift refers to a fundamental change in pattern or underlying model.

Explanation: Vulnerabilities means state of being exposed to harm; opposite is Defenses or Strengths.

Q11. Read the sentence to find whether there is any grammatical error in it:

Q13. Read the sentence to find whether there is any grammatical error in it:

[Directions Q31-Q35]: Study the table showing applicants and selected candidate percentages in 5 Public Sector Banks in 2025:
- SBI: 120,000 Total Applicants | 5% Selected
- PNB: 80,000 Total Applicants | 4% Selected
- BOB: 90,000 Total Applicants | 6% Selected
- CANARA: 60,000 Total Applicants | 5% Selected
- UNION: 50,000 Total Applicants | 8% Selected

Q31. What is the total number of selected candidates in SBI and BOB together?
(A) 11,400
(B) 12,000
(C) 10,800
(D) 11,200
(E) 11,800
Correct Option: A
Explanation: SBI selected = 120,000 * 5% = 6,000. BOB selected = 90,000 * 6% = 5,400. Total = 11,400.

Q32. Find the ratio of selected candidates in PNB to CANARA.
(A) 16 : 15
(B) 15 : 16
(C) 4 : 3
(D) 3 : 4
(E) 5 : 4
Correct Option: A
Explanation: PNB selected = 80,000 * 4% = 3,200. CANARA selected = 60,000 * 5% = 3,000. Ratio = 3200 : 3000 = 16 : 15.

Q33. Total applicants in UNION Bank is what percentage less than total applicants in PNB?
(A) 37.5%
(B) 40.0%
(C) 35.0%
(D) 30.0%
(E) 42.5%
Correct Option: A
Explanation: Difference = 80,000 - 50,000 = 30,000. Percentage less = (30,000 / 80,000) * 100 = 37.5%.

Q34. If 45% of selected candidates in SBI are female, find the number of male candidates selected in SBI.
(A) 3,300
(B) 2,700
(C) 3,600
(D) 3,100
(E) 3,450
Correct Option: A
Explanation: SBI total selected = 6,000. Male selected = 55% of 6,000 = 3,300.

Q35. Find the average number of selected candidates across all 5 banks.
(A) 3,920
(B) 4,000
(C) 3,850
(D) 4,100
(E) 3,750
Correct Option: A
Explanation: Selected sum = 6000 + 3200 + 5400 + 3000 + 4000 = 19,600. Avg = 19,600 / 5 = 3,920.

Q41. Solve equations and establish relation between x and y:
I. x² - 13x + 40 = 0
II. y² - 17y + 70 = 0
(A) x > y
(B) x < y
(C) x ≥ y
(D) x ≤ y
(E) x = y or relation cannot be established
Correct Option: E
Explanation: x = 5, 8. y = 7, 10. Since 5 < 7 and 8 > 7, relation cannot be established.

Q46. What should come in place of question mark (?) in the series: 12, 18, 36, 90, 270, ?
(A) 945
(B) 810
(C) 1080
(D) 720
(E) 990
Correct Option: A
Explanation: Pattern: ×1.5, ×2.0, ×2.5, ×3.0, ×3.5. Next: 270 × 3.5 = 945.

[Directions Q66-Q70]: 8 persons (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H) live on an 8-story building numbered 1 to 8 from bottom to top:
- A lives on an even numbered floor above floor 4.
- Only 3 persons live between A and B.
- C lives immediately below B.
- E lives on an odd numbered floor below C.
- Two persons live between E and F.
- H lives above G but below D.

Q66. Who among the following lives on the 8th floor?
(A) A
(B) D
(C) H
(D) F
(E) E
Correct Option: A
Explanation: Floor arrangement: Floor 8: A, Floor 7: D, Floor 6: H, Floor 5: G, Floor 4: B, Floor 3: C, Floor 2: F, Floor 1: E.

Q67. How many persons live between A and C?
(A) 4
(B) 3
(C) 2
(D) 1
(E) 5
Correct Option: A
Explanation: A is on floor 8, C on floor 3. Between them are floors 7, 6, 5, 4 (4 persons: D, H, G, B).

Q86. Statements:
Only a few Bank are PO.
All PO are Exam.
No Exam is Easy.

Conclusions:
I. Some Bank can never be Easy.
II. All PO being Bank is a possibility.
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows
(E) Both conclusions I and II follow
Correct Option: E
Explanation: C1 follows (Bank portion in PO is inside Exam, not Easy). C2 follows (Only a few Bank are PO allows all PO to be Bank). Both follow.

Q93. Statements: M ≥ N > O = P ≤ Q < R
Conclusions:
I. M > P
II. R > O
(A) Only conclusion I follows
(B) Only conclusion II follows
(C) Either conclusion I or II follows
(D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows
(E) Both conclusions I and II follow
Correct Option: E
Explanation: M > P holds from M ≥ N > O = P. R > O holds from O = P ≤ Q < R. Both follow.
